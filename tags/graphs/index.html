(4,7) buckets

One of these days a friend mentioned this internet puzzle, which can be stated as follows: You have two buckets. One bucket with capacity of 4 L, and another with 7 L. Using only these two buckets, you are asked to obtain exactly 6 L of water. The problem also establishes two rules, namely: You have infinite water, and can waste it as much as you want. You can move water from one bucket to another, but only in a way that at least one of the two buckets ends up either empty or full. Although we could inspect a solution using basic arithmetic reasoning, we can also think of this puzzle as an exercise in graph theory.
